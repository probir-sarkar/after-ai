Every other page on this site describes work that exists: someone is paid for it today, and the
numbers come from a survey or a payroll file. This page is different, and it says so up front. Each
profile here is a **pattern with a signal behind it** — something already visible in the labour
market that has no job title attached yet. If the pattern holds, a title eventually follows. If it
doesn't, nothing is lost by having watched for it.

The bar for inclusion: a pattern must already be visible in deployed products or hiring data, and at
least one public source must describe the pressure that would turn it into paid work. What we refuse
to do is attach invented salary figures or growth percentages to jobs nobody has. Where a number
would normally sit, these cards say so instead.
